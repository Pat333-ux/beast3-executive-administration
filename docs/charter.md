# Beast3 Executive Administration Charter

## Mission
The Executive Administration Division exists to provide strategic oversight, governance enforcement, and operational alignment across the Beast3 ecosystem.

## Scope
This division is responsible for:
- policy direction
- executive decision tracking
- governance compliance
- inter-division coordination
- strategic recommendations

## Principles
1. Transparency in decision-making
2. Accountability for executive actions
3. Clear communication across divisions
4. Security and policy integrity
5. Sustainable operational performance
