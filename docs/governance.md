# Governance Framework

## Governance Structure
The governance framework defines how authority flows through the Executive Administration Division and supporting divisions.

## Roles
- Executive Council
- Policy Engine
- Division Registry
- Executive Directive Manager
- Audit and performance oversight

## Decision Model
Decisions pass through a vetted approval cycle that includes:
- proposal intake
- policy review
- executive alignment
- implementation acknowledgment

## Compliance Standards
The administration enforces a minimal standard of record retention, operational traceability, and transparent review.
