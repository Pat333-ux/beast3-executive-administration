# Division Structure

The Beast3 ecosystem is organized to support mission execution, operational integrity, and strategic governance. The Executive Administration Division coordinates with the broader network of divisions and functional stakeholders.

## Core Divisions
- Executive Administration
- Strategy and Planning
- Finance and Resourcing
- Security and Risk
- Operations and Infrastructure
- Policy and Compliance
- Communications and Coordination

## Administration Model
Each division remains accountable to the same executive principles: traceability, measurability, and structured governance.
